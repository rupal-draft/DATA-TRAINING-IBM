# Scenario 3: Sentiment & Keyword Frequency Analysis

## Task:
Identify the most frequently used words in Darth Vader's dialogue across the entire trilogy to see if his vocabulary is consistently "dark."

## Instructions:
* Combine the Data: Use the UNION operator to merge the data from all three files (episodeIV_dialogues.txt, episodeV_dialogues.txt, and episodeVI_dialogues.txt) into one large dataset.
* Isolate the Character: Filter the dataset so you only have lines where the character is "VADER".
* Break Down the Sentences: Use the TOKENIZE function on the dialogue column to break sentences into individual words.
* Count and Rank: Group by the word, count the occurrences, and sort them to see Vader's top 10 most used words.
